Cookie Policy

Effective date: 2026-05-27 · Last updated: 2026-05-27

This Cookie Policy explains how cookies, local storage (localStorage / sessionStorage) and similar technologies are used in the Zirve Toptan mobile app, web admin panel and the toptanperakende.online website.

1. TL;DR

We use no marketing or tracking cookies. No Google Analytics, no Facebook Pixel, no ad re-targeting. Only a handful of strictly technical items are used to keep the app working.

2. What is a cookie?

A cookie is a small piece of data a website places on your device through your browser. Cookies help recognize you on subsequent visits or remember preferences. In mobile apps the same role is played by "local storage."

3. Technologies we use

TechnologyPurposeTypeLifetime
Session token (JWT) Access & refresh JWTs are stored in secure local storage to keep your session. Strictly necessary Access: 30 min · Refresh: 14 days
Preference store UI preferences like language and theme are stored in local storage. Strictly necessary Until the user clears it
Flutter service worker (web admin) Caches Flutter assets for faster subsequent loads (no offline mode). Strictly necessary Subject to browser cache policy
Caddy / HTTPS session The server may use small session cookies for HTTP/2 + TLS management. Strictly necessary Session

4. Cookies we do NOT use

  • Advertising cookies (DoubleClick, Facebook Pixel, TikTok, Google Ads, AdSense, etc.) — none.
  • Analytics cookies (Google Analytics, Mixpanel, Amplitude, Hotjar, Plausible, Segment, etc.) — none.
  • Social media cookies (Facebook Like, Twitter button, etc.) — none.
  • CRM / re-marketing trackers — none.

5. Third-party cookies

Zirve Toptan does not allow third parties to set cookies through the site. The mobile app contains no third-party advertising or tracking SDKs.

6. Managing your cookie preferences

  • From the browser: Delete or disable cookies from your browser settings. Doing so signs you out — you'll need to sign in again.
  • From the mobile app: Clear Zirve Toptan's storage from your device's system settings. The saved session is cleared — sign in again.
  • From your account: Delete your account to remove all server-side data within 14 days and backups within 30 days. See Account & Data Deletion.

7. Policy updates

When this Cookie Policy changes, the "Last updated" date is bumped. If a new technology needs to be added (e.g. opt-in analytics), it will be announced on this page first and your consent will be requested.
